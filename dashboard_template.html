ℹ️ Methodological Note on MD&A Word Counts & SEC Modernization
  • Raw MD&A Words: Total word count extracted directly from SEC Form 10-K Item 7 (Management's Discussion & Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations).
  • Cleaned MD&A Words: Non-stopword tokens evaluated by the Loughran-McDonald Financial Sentiment Dictionary after stop-word, numeral, and punctuation filtering (~50–60% of raw words).
  • SEC Item 303 Modernization: In FY2019/FY2020, Apple adopted SEC FAST Act amendments allowing the omission of the earliest comparative fiscal period if previously disclosed, condensing Item 7 from 38 pages to 6 pages. Microsoft retained its extensive segment discussions, maintaining 7,200–11,500 raw words.
